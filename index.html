Reclamation

Sector 12 was decommissioned eleven years ago. The coolant was drained, the crews were paid off, and nobody remembered to switch off the defence grid. You have been contracted to walk in and clear it, room by room, so the salvage teams can follow. Three rooms. Seven waves. The machines do not know the site is closed.

  1. Click the picture to take control of the mouse
  2. Move the mouse to look and aim
  3. W A S D to walk — W forward, S back, A and D sideways
  4. Left click to shoot
  5. Scroll wheel to change weapon

Space jumps · C crouches · R reloads · Esc frees the mouse · Tab for all keys
